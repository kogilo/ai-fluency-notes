## Apply the 4Ds: Communication Project

**Scenario:** Working with an AI assistant to draft a series of emails for a marketing campaign.

### Delegation: *what I handle vs. what AI helps with*
- **I handle:**
  - **Campaign goals**, **target audience**, and **success metrics**
  - The **core message** and brand positioning
  - **Final approval** of every email before it goes out
- **I collaborate with AI on:**
  - **Brainstorming** subject lines and angles
  - **Drafting** first versions of each email
  - **Variations** for A/B testing
  - *Tightening* copy and checking consistency across the series
- **Rule of thumb:** AI helps with **drafting and iteration**. I own the **strategy and the final voice**.

### Description: *communicating my vision*
- Give the AI **rich context up front**:
  - **Purpose:** what the campaign is for and what action readers should take
  - **Audience:** who they are, what they care about, and their pain points
  - **Tone and style:** e.g., *warm, professional, no jargon*, with sample emails or phrases I like
  - **Format:** length, structure, and call to action for each email
  - **Success criteria:** e.g., *clear, persuasive, and consistent across the series*
- Say what to **avoid** (clichés, hype, exaggerated claims).
- Work in **loops**: *describe, review, give feedback, refine*.

### Discernment: *evaluating the drafts*
- **Accuracy:** are the **facts, features, and claims** true?
- **Tone:** does it **sound like my brand**, not generic AI copy?
- **Audience fit:** would my **target reader** care about this?
- **Clarity:** is the **message and call to action** obvious?
- **Consistency:** does the series **flow together** with no repeated or contradictory points?
- **Overall:** does it **move the campaign forward**, or does it need refining or discarding?

### Diligence: *transparency and responsibility*
- **Verify** every claim, statistic, and promise before sending.
- **Protect data:** never paste **customer information or confidential details** into the AI.
- **Check compliance** with marketing rules (e.g., *honest advertising, unsubscribe options, privacy laws*).
- **Be transparent:** follow my organization's policy on **disclosing AI involvement**, and don't pretend AI-written content is personal correspondence.
- **Take ownership:** I **review every email** and **stand behind** what is sent under my name.
