## Introduction to AI Fluency

- The course is about **how humans interact and collaborate with AI systems**, imagining AI as a _trusted partner_ for **creative and innovative problem-solving**, and approaching it **confidently and capably**.
- Goal: engage with AI in ways that are **effective, efficient, ethical, and safe**, which means _fundamentally shifting how we approach AI_.
- **The AI Fluency Framework** is a model for human-AI interaction built on **four core competencies, the 4 Ds**:
  - **Delegation:** when should humans do the work, and when should AI?
  - **Description:** how do we communicate clearly with AI systems?
  - **Discernment:** how do we evaluate what AI gives us?
  - **Diligence:** how do we ensure our AI interaction is _responsible, transparent, and accountable_?
- Each section pairs **focused videos with exercises and materials**. _Practice what you learn._
- Most AI training focuses on **tactical skills** (specific prompts, settings, tools) that **quickly become outdated**. This course focuses on **core competencies and deep understanding** to prepare for _future changes in AI_, not just today's.
- **By the end, you'll have:**
  - A **framework** to guide your own AI interactions
  - **Confidence** in deciding _when and how_ to engage AI effectively
  - **Practical skills** for more thoughtful, fluid human-AI collaboration
  - The ability to **evaluate and take responsibility** for the outcomes
