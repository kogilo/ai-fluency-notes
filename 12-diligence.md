## The Description-Discernment Loop

- **Goal:** apply **Description and Discernment** to a **real project** and use the **feedback loop** between them to get results that *exceed what either human or AI could achieve alone*.

### Step 1: Review Your Project Plan
- Pull up the **project plan from Lesson 5**.
- Quickly review your **delegation decisions**: which tasks need **human expertise**, which suit **AI capabilities**, and which call for **collaboration**.
- Feel free to **refine the plan** based on what you've learned since.

### Step 2: Prepare Your Description Approach
- Start a conversation with Claude, **explain the project**, and plan your Description *before* executing.
- **Product description:** what **outputs** do you need for each task? *Format, style, length, and level of detail*.
- **Process description:** how should Claude **approach** each task? *Specific methods, frameworks, or steps*.
- **Performance description:** what **collaborative behavior** do you want?
  - **Concise or detailed**
  - **Challenging or supportive**
  - Focused on **ideas or analysis**
- Discuss these with Claude to **establish clear expectations** for the collaboration.

### Step 3: Execute with Description-Discernment Loops
- For **each task** in the project:
  1. **Describe** what you need clearly:
     - **Product:** what you want
     - **Process:** how Claude should approach or think about the task
     - **Performance:** how Claude should engage with you
  2. **Discern** the quality of what you receive:
     - **Product discernment:** evaluate the *output itself*
     - **Process discernment:** assess *how Claude approached the task*
     - **Performance discernment:** consider *whether Claude's behavior is most helpful*
  3. **Refine** based on your discernment:
     - Give feedback on **what worked and what didn't**
     - **Clarify or adjust** your description
     - Request **iterations** until you're satisfied
  4. **Integrate your own expertise and judgment:**
     - Add your **unique perspective, creativity, or domain knowledge**
     - Make the **final decisions** on what to *keep, modify, or discard*
     - **Take responsibility** for the final output
- **Repeat the loop** for each task until the project is complete.

### Reflection Prompts
- What **patterns** did I notice in the descriptions that led to the **best outcomes**?
- Which required **more effort**, **Description or Discernment**, and why?
- How did my **actual execution compare to the Lesson 5 plan**, and what **adjustments** did I make along the way?
