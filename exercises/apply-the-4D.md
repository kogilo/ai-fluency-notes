## Apply the 4Ds: Communication Project

**Scenario:** Working with an AI assistant to draft a series of emails for a marketing campaign.

### Delegation: _what I handle vs. what AI helps with_

- **I handle:**
  - **Campaign goals**, **target audience**, and **success metrics**
  - The **core message** and brand positioning
  - **Final approval** of every email before it goes out
- **I collaborate with AI on:**
  - **Brainstorming** subject lines and angles
  - **Drafting** first versions of each email
  - **Variations** for A/B testing
  - _Tightening_ copy and checking consistency across the series
- **Rule of thumb:** AI helps with **drafting and iteration**. I own the **strategy and the final voice**.

### Description: _communicating my vision_

- Give the AI **rich context up front**:
  - **Purpose:** what the campaign is for and what action readers should take
  - **Audience:** who they are, what they care about, and their pain points
  - **Tone and style:** e.g., _warm, professional, no jargon_, with sample emails or phrases I like
  - **Format:** length, structure, and call to action for each email
  - **Success criteria:** e.g., _clear, persuasive, and consistent across the series_
- Say what to **avoid** (clichés, hype, exaggerated claims).
- Work in **loops**: _describe, review, give feedback, refine_.

### Discernment: _evaluating the drafts_

- **Accuracy:** are the **facts, features, and claims** true?
- **Tone:** does it **sound like my brand**, not generic AI copy?
- **Audience fit:** would my **target reader** care about this?
