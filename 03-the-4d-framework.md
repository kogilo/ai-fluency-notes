## What Is AI Fluency?

- **AI is reshaping** how we communicate, create, learn, and solve problems, but *having access to AI doesn't mean we know how to use it well or responsibly*. There is a gap between **having access** and being **truly fluent**.
- **AI fluency** is not about being a technical expert or memorizing the "10 best prompts." It's a collection of **practical skills, knowledge, insights, and values** that reinforce each other and *adapt as the technology changes*.
- At its heart, AI fluency means interacting with AI in ways that are **effective, efficient, ethical, and safe**: getting the most out of AI *without wasting time and energy*, and doing it *honestly and responsibly*, protecting the **privacy and security** of yourself and others.

### Three Ways We Engage with AI

- **Automation:** AI completes a **specific task** based on your instructions (summarize a document, draft an email, create an image, plan a trip).
  - You define *what* needs to be done, and the AI executes it.
  - Works well with a **clear outcome in mind**, but is harder when you're *not sure what you're looking for*.
- **Augmentation:** you and the AI **collaborate on a task together**. The AI is a *creative thinking and problem-solving partner*, not a machine.
  - Examples: developing a story character, working through an architectural problem in an app, shaping thoughts on a research topic.
  - The AI *doesn't do the work for you, but helps you do your work better*.
  - Best when **solutions aren't straightforward** and you need space to explore and experiment.
- **Agency:** AI **works independently on your behalf**.
  - Examples: categorizing and drafting replies to incoming emails, an ever-changing interactive website experience, a dynamic character in a game.
  - You establish the AI's **knowledge and behavior patterns** rather than specific actions, *less like a script writer, more like a director setting a vision*.

### Key Takeaways

- **No mode is inherently better**. Each serves different purposes, and you might use **all three in a single project**.
- Many people start with automation, but **augmentation and agency** take the most advantage of AI's unique capabilities and often lead to the **most creative and effective solutions**.
- AI is **more than a tool**. It can be a **tool, a medium, a partner, or a co-creator**, sometimes all at once. This shift means we have **new roles** and need to **develop new skills**.
- Those skills make up the **AI Fluency Framework**: **four key areas of competence** to develop and master, whichever way you engage with AI.
